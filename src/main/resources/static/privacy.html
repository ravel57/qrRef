qrRef Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes what data the Android app qrRef (package ru.ravel.qrref, the "App") processes and how it is used. Developer: Petr Lomakin (the "Developer"). Contact: petyalom99@gmail.com.

1. What the App does

qrRef lets you transfer text, links and files between devices. You scan a QR code shown on another device (for example, on the qrref.io website) and send data to it, or you receive data sent to your device. You can also open a chat with another device and exchange messages and files.

2. Data we process

2.1. Content you send

Text, links, file names and file contents, and chat messages that you send through the App are transmitted to the qrRef server (by default https://qrref.io) solely to deliver them to the recipient: the device whose QR code you scanned, or your chat partner. Data addressed to you is received from the server and displayed on screen; received files are saved to the Downloads folder on your device.

2.2. Device key

On first launch the App generates a random 20-character key that identifies your device for receiving data. The key is not linked to your name, phone number, email address or advertising ID. It is stored locally on the device and sent to the server so that data can be delivered to you. When devices are paired (a "bridge" or chat is created), the server temporarily links the two keys into one room.

2.3. Camera

The App requests camera access only to scan QR codes. Camera images are processed on the device in real time and are not stored or sent to the server. Only the key read from the QR code is sent to the server.

2.4. Data we do NOT collect

  • name, phone number, email address or accounts — the App has no registration;
  • location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or photos and videos from your gallery (only files you choose yourself or send via the system "Share" menu are transferred);
  • advertising identifiers or data for advertising;
  • usage analytics or crash reports — the App contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers.

3. Local storage on your device

The App stores only settings on the device: the server address, the device key, the pairing key, the "open links automatically" option and the theme. They are kept in the App's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Because Android backup is enabled for the App, these settings may be included in your Google account's automatic backup, depending on your device settings.

4. Server and retention

Data is transmitted over secure connections (HTTPS/WSS). The server acts as an intermediary: transferred texts and files are kept only for as long as needed to deliver them to the recipient and are then deleted. Chat history is not retained on the server after the chat is closed. We do not use the content you transfer for advertising, do not sell it and do not analyze it.

File download links contain a random secret component; do not share them if you do not want others to obtain the file. Do not send data through the App that you are not willing to pass through an intermediary internet service (passwords, payment details, etc.).

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with third parties, except where required by law (in response to a valid request from authorized authorities). The only recipient of your data is the device you chose yourself. The server's hosting provider processes data for technical purposes as an infrastructure provider.

6. Custom server

In the settings you can specify the address of your own qrRef server. In that case data is sent to the server you specified, its operator is solely responsible for how it is handled, and this policy does not apply to that server.

7. App permissions

  • Camera — scanning QR codes;
  • Internet — exchanging data with the qrRef server;
  • The App can accept content through the system "Share" menu — only when you yourself choose qrRef in that menu.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Your rights and choices

At any time you can close a chat, clear the list of received items, clear the App's data or uninstall it. This deletes all local data, including the device key (a new key is generated at the next launch). To request deletion of data on the server or to ask any other question, contact petyalom99@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, and the date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
